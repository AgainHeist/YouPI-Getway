Mathematical Payment Invariants

How Sovereign Bank Observation Works

Standard P2P UPI bank alerts do not carry merchant metadata. YOUPI PG solves payment attribution deterministically with four strict engineering invariants.

STEP 01 · IDENTITY

WhatsApp OTP Pre-Gate

Buyers authenticate their 10-digit Indian mobile number via 6-digit WhatsApp OTP before entering the payment queue — stopping bots and fake orders cold.

STEP 02 · FIFO ARBITER

Exclusive Single-Slot VPA

Only one active buyer occupies a receiving VPA at a time (5:00 window). Concurrent buyers wait in an atomic SQLite FIFO queue with live SSE updates.

STEP 03 · VECTOR QR

ISO-18004 Pure SVG QR

Zero third-party QR APIs. Generates crisp vector UPI QR codes & 1-tap mobile deep-links for GPay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM, and CRED in sub-millisecond time.

STEP 04 · MESH VERIFY

Tri-Channel Bank Mesh

Native Android SMS/Notification APK + Gmail OAuth2 + Google Messages parse incoming bank credits, deduplicate UTRs, and dispatch HMAC-SHA256 webhooks.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know

How does YOUPI PG verify UPI payments without aggregator APIs? +
YOUPI PG pairs an exclusive single-slot FIFO queue per receiving VPA with a tri-channel observation mesh (Native Android SMS/Notification APK, Gmail OAuth2 API, and Google Messages). When your bank sends a credit alert matching the active slot's exact integer Paise amount, the Attribution Engine extracts the 12-digit UTR, marks the session SUCCESS, and dispatches a signed webhook.
How do I test the live checkout right here on this website? +
Click Try ₹1 Demo Checkout at the top of this page (or visit /store). Enter any 10-digit Indian mobile number, click 1-Tap Auto-Fill & Verify on the sandbox OTP banner, and click Simulate Bank SMS Credit to watch real-time UTR attribution and webhook settlement!
Can I customize the checkout UI with my own brand logo and colors? +
Yes! Open the Merchant Admin Hub (/admin), use the 1-Tap Demo Unlock in sandbox mode (or your ADMIN_API_TOKEN in self-hosted mode), and configure your app name, logo URL, primary color, Online/Offline selling mode, and custom receiving UPI IDs.
Should I fulfill orders from browser events or server webhooks? +
Browser youpi:payment:success events are strictly for instant UI feedback. Always fulfill orders on your backend after verifying the raw-body X-Gateway-Signature (HMAC-SHA256) webhook and checking payload.method === "UPI".
